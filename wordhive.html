    HOW TO PLAY

    Make as many words as you can from the 7 letters in the hive.

    • Words must be at least 4 letters long.
    • Every word must use the centre letter.
    • Letters can be used more than once.
    • Names, places and offensive words don't count.

    Scoring: 4-letter words are worth 1 point. Longer words earn 1 point per letter. Every puzzle has at least one pangram — a word that uses all 7 letters — worth 7 bonus points.

    Real but uncommon words are accepted as bonus words; they're shown in your list but don't count toward your rank.

    Climb the ranks up to Genius, or find every word to become Hive Master. Submit your score to the arcade leaderboard whenever you're done for the day. A new hive arrives every midnight.

    Tap the letters or type on your keyboard. Enter submits, Backspace deletes, Space shuffles.
