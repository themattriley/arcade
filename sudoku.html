HOW TO PLAY

Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

  • Tap a square, then tap a number (or type it).
  • Notes mode lets you pencil in small candidate numbers.
  • Undo steps back; Erase clears a square; Hint reveals one square.
  • The small number under each key shows how many are still to place.

Scoring: each difficulty starts at a base score (Easy 1,000 · Medium 2,000 · Hard 3,500 · Expert 5,000). You lose 1 point for every 2 seconds, 150 per mistake and 300 per hint, down to a minimum of 100.

Keyboard: 1–9 to enter, arrows to move, Backspace to erase, N for notes, Z to undo, P to pause.

Your game saves automatically — close the tab and pick up where you left off.
