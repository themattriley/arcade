HOW TO PLAY

Find groups of four words that share something in common.

  • Tap four words, then press Submit to check your guess.
  • Find all four groups without making 4 mistakes.
  • If you're one word off, you'll be told you're one away.

Each puzzle has exactly one solution. Watch out for words that seem to fit more than one group!
